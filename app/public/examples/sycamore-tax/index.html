FAQ

Questions we get a lot

What should I bring?

Our what-to-bring list covers the usual paperwork. If you're not sure about something, bring it or give us a call.

Can I drop off my paperwork?

Yes. Call ahead and we'll tell you how drop-off works.

Are you open after tax season?

After tax season we're open Monday through Thursday, 9 to 4, and by appointment.

How do I send documents?

Use our secure client portal. Please don't email tax documents or send them through the contact form.

¿Hablan español?

Sí. Llámenos y con gusto le ayudamos en español.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are easiest, but you can also drop off your paperwork. Give us a call and we'll find a time that works.
