Your first Sunday

It's normal to wonder what to expect at a new church. Here are a few answers before you come.

  • Where to park

    Park anywhere in the front lot. The main doors face the road, and a greeter will meet you.

  • What to wear

    Some folks wear a suit and some wear jeans. Come as you are comfortable.

  • Your kids

    Nursery for babies through age 3 during Sunday School and worship.

  • How long it lasts

    Morning worship usually runs about an hour.

Ministries

Something for everyone

There's a place here for every age, from little ones to senior adults.

  • Sunday School & Bible classes

    Classes for every age before morning worship.

  • Kids

    A place for children to learn and make friends.

  • Students

    Middle and high schoolers growing together.

  • Women

    Women of all ages meeting to study and serve.

  • Men

    Men gathering for fellowship and service.

  • Music & choir

    Anyone who loves to sing is welcome to join in.

About us

About Cedar Creek Baptist Church

Cedar Creek Baptist Church is a small country church just outside Cullman, where neighbors gather on Sundays and Wednesday nights.

Whether you've been in church all your life or it's been a while, you're welcome here.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot

What time are services?

Sunday 9:45 AM: Sunday School. Sunday 11:00 AM: Morning Worship. Sunday 6:00 PM: Evening Worship. Wednesday 6:30 PM: Prayer & Bible Study.

Where do I park?

Park anywhere in the front lot. The main doors face the road, and a greeter will meet you.

What should I wear?

Some folks wear a suit and some wear jeans. Come as you are comfortable.

What about my kids?

Nursery for babies through age 3 during Sunday School and worship.

Can I watch online?

Yes. Tap Watch live to join us online.

Who's welcome?

Everyone. Whether you're new to Cullman or just looking for a church home, we'd be glad to have you. Give us a call with any questions.
